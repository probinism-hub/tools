Frequently asked questions

Where do the keyword ideas come from?

The tool asks the selected search engine for its autocomplete suggestions many times, using your seed keyword with letters, numbers, question words and common modifiers added. All the results are merged, de-duplicated and ranked.

Does it show search volume or keyword difficulty?

No. Those numbers come from paid keyword databases, and autocomplete does not provide them. This tool shows a relevance score instead. Use the Trends button on any row, or your preferred SEO tool, to check demand for the keywords you shortlist.

Why do I get fewer results than expected?

Very niche seed keywords have fewer suggestions. Try a broader term, switch to Deep search depth, or set Seed match to Any suggestion. If nothing loads at all, an ad blocker or network filter may be blocking the suggestion service.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Lookups run directly from your browser to the search engine. Nothing is sent to or saved on this website.
