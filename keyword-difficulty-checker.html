    How is the score calculated?

    Quick estimate: based on the keyword itself (length, commercial intent words, question words). This runs entirely in your browser, so it is a rough guide only and does not use live Google data.

    Accurate mode: uses the authority and backlinks of the pages you enter from Google's top 10, plus weak spots (forums, small sites) and SERP features. Scores are estimates and will differ from paid tools like Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz.
