How is the score calculated?

Quick Estimate reads the keyword itself: how many words it has, whether it contains commercial or informational wording, and whether it belongs to a highly competitive topic such as finance, insurance or hosting. It does not use live Google data, so treat it as a fast first filter.

SERP Analysis uses the real results you enter. Higher positions count more, then the score is adjusted for snippets, ads, big brands and weak pages. It also compares the result with your own site's authority.

Is this the same as Ahrefs or Semrush difficulty?

No. Those tools use their own proprietary backlink databases. This tool gives a transparent, independent estimate you can use to compare keywords and prioritise your content. For high-stakes decisions, confirm with a paid tool.
