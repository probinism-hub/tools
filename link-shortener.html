    Frequently asked questions

    Do my short links expire?

    Links are created by third-party services (is.gd, v.gd, TinyURL). Their policies decide how long a link lasts, so keep your original URL somewhere safe for important links.

    Can I see how many people clicked my link?

    This tool does not track clicks. Some services offer basic statistics on their own websites, but this tool does not connect to them.

    Where is my link history stored?

    Only in your own browser on this device. We do not keep a copy. Clearing your browser data will remove it.

    Why did my custom name not work?

    Custom names must be unique. If someone already uses that name, try a different one. TinyURL does not support custom names here, so choose is.gd or v.gd.
