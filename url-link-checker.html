URL Link Checker

Find out where a link is used. Enter any URL, then scan your website or a list of pages to see exactly which pages link to it, with anchor text and nofollow status.

The tool reads the website's sitemap (for example /sitemap.xml or WordPress's /wp-sitemap.xml) to get the page list, then opens each page and looks for your URL.

Paste any pages, on any website, that you think might link to your URL. Up to 500 pages.

Open ready-made searches on major search engines to discover pages across the web that mention your URL. Then copy the pages you find into Check specific pages to confirm each one really links to you.

Good to know: a search engine shows only a small sample of the links pointing to a page, and these searches are not a complete backlink report. For a fuller picture of your own site, use the Links report in Google Search Console, or a dedicated backlink service that runs its own web crawler.
